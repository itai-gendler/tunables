# Adopt tunables in an app

## Goal

An app that keeps its tunable values in code, in its environment or in a table
of its own reaches one home for each, declared once, stored in its own database
and changeable while it runs.

## Success criterion

The skills are installed, the Developer approved what became a setting and what
stayed a deployment value, every group is stored at its defaults, and the
definition checks pass.
